DocuChat

Ask questions. Get answers grounded in your documents.

DocuChat is a “chat with your documents” backend built around the retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pattern. Users upload text documents and ask an LLM questions about their contents, so responses can be grounded in relevant source material rather than relying only on general model knowledge.

THE CORE IDEA

Retrieve relevant document context, then use that context to help generate a response.

AI-Powered Task Management

Less time organizing tasks. More time doing meaningful work.

Our group proposed an AI-powered task management application that helps people turn natural-language requests into structured tasks, organize work, and understand what may need attention next.

01

Natural-language capture
Extract task details such as title, due date, project, and priority.

02

Context-aware assistance
Suggest priorities, categories, and useful task summaries.

03

Search and briefings
Find tasks by meaning and surface overdue or upcoming work.

RESPONSIBLE DESIGN

Suggestions should remain editable. Users should be able to correct AI mistakes, manage privacy, and continue with basic task management if AI is unavailable.
