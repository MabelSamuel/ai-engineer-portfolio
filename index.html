Backend · RAG

DocuChat

Ask questions and get answers based on your own documents.

DocuChat is a "chat with your documents" backend built on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Users upload text documents and ask an LLM about them, so answers draw on the documents themselves, not just the model's general knowledge.

The core idea

First retrieve the relevant parts of the document, then give them to the model so it can write the answer.

Project 02

Flow

Capture. Prioritize. Act.

Product concept · In progress

AI-Powered Task Management

Spend less time organizing tasks and more time on the work itself.

An AI-powered task manager that turns natural-language requests into structured tasks, organizes the work, and points out what may need attention next.

Natural-language capture

Pulls out task details such as the title, due date, project and priority.

Context-aware assistance

Suggests priorities, categories and short task summaries.

Search and briefings

Finds tasks by meaning, not just keywords, and highlights overdue or upcoming work.

Responsible design

Users can edit every suggestion and correct the AI's mistakes. They control their privacy, and the basic task features still work if the AI is unavailable.

  • LLM-assisted workflows
  • Task prioritization
  • Human oversight
